# E-Bow and Sustain

## Framing

Use E-Bow as a sustain and texture instrument, not only a novelty sound.

The useful roles are:

- drone bed
- slow counterline
- transition texture
- harmonic lift
- contrast against picked rhythm guitar

## Core exercises

### Drone bed

Create a stable tonal center under changing guitar fragments.

Spec:

- one root or fifth drone
- 4 to 8 bars
- slow filter or volume movement
- no melodic clutter

### Counterline

Add a sustained upper voice against a bassline or chord vamp.

Spec:

- 3 to 5 notes total
- mostly stepwise motion
- leave gaps between phrases
- resolve at section boundaries

### Transition texture

Use E-Bow to glue two song sections together.

Spec:

- enter two bars before the transition
- swell into the downbeat
- hold through the first bar of the next section
- fade behind the next groove

## Practice constraints

- Record against a simple bass/drum loop
- Avoid constant pitch movement
- Treat silence as part of the phrase
- Keep the harmonic role clear: root, fifth, suspension, or leading tone

## Example practice specification

```text
Style lane: ambient rock
Focus: E-Bow sustain and controlled transitions
Tasks: drone bed, sparse counterline, transition texture
Backing context: bass and drums only
Evidence: one isolated sustain check and one musical-context take
Space: leave explicit gaps between counterline phrases
```
