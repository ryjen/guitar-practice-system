## Country branch selection

Do not select a country song merely to populate the catalog. Choose a legally sourced passage when one of these technique needs becomes active:

- alternating bass
- hybrid-picked double-stops
- major/minor pentatonic contrast
- pedal-steel-style bends
- fills between vocal phrases

Reference `country-clean-diagnostic` or `country-clean-musical` and the planned `country-i-iv-v` or `country-rock-form` backing assets.

## Experimental instrument use cases

The electric cigar-box banjo may support original roots, drone, slide, or arrangement studies. Prefer self-created material so tuning, phrasing, and backing tracks can be committed without copyright ambiguity.
