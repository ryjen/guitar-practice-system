# Jazz Background Generator Prompt

```text
Generate a jazz-coloured background practice scaffold for my guitar practice system.

Primary style center:
- wah / slide / E-Bow / delay / atmospheric guitar

Jazz role:
- secondary vocabulary source, not a separate jazz curriculum
- use jazz for harmony, rhythm, phrasing, substitutions, comping ideas, and voice-leading
- keep the material playable and useful for my core sound

Input:
- Core sound: {{core_sound}}
- Jazz ingredient: {{jazz_ingredient}}
- Tempo / feel: {{tempo_feel}}
- Key / mode: {{key_mode}}
- Guitar role: {{guitar_role}}
- Bass role: {{bass_role}}
- Drum feel: {{drum_feel}}
- DAW target: {{daw_target}}

Output a practice-ready backing spec with:
1. Title
2. Intent
3. Core sound
4. Tempo and meter
5. Key center
6. Chord loop or section map
7. Bass direction
8. Drum direction
9. Guitar practice role
10. Constraints
11. Four useful variations
12. What to generate next

Rules:
- Keep the wah / slide / E-Bow style as the center of gravity
- Use jazz colour without turning this into a full jazz-standard study
- Prefer fragments over complete songs
- Leave room for phrasing, sustain, silence, and texture
- Make the harmony interesting but not busy
- Prioritize rhythm, sound, sustain, and feel
- Avoid fast bebop-line assumptions unless explicitly requested
- Make the first version loopable
```
