# Jazz-Funk Wah Comping Background

## Intent

Create a rhythm-focused jazz-funk background for practicing wah timing, muted strums, syncopated comping, and short chord stabs.

## Core sound

Controlled, pocket-oriented wah rhythm over jazz-coloured harmony. Not a full funk workout and not a jazz-standard study.

## Settings

- Tempo: 96 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Key center: E minor / G major colour
- Feel: tight sixteenth-note grid, slightly behind the beat
- Guitar role: wah comping, muted rhythm, short partial-chord stabs
- Bass role: syncopated root / octave movement
- Drums: closed-hat funk groove with restrained fills

## Chord loop

| Bar | Harmony | Function |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Em9 | minor tonic colour |
| 2 | A13 | dominant lift |
| 3 | Dmaj9 | soft resolution |
| 4 | Gmaj7 | major colour |
| 5 | Cmaj9 | wider lift |
| 6 | F#7#9 | altered dominant bite |
| 7 | Bm9 | minor landing |
| 8 | B7alt | turnaround tension |

## Bass direction

- Lock with kick drum
- Use root / octave movement as the default
- Add short chromatic approaches into Em9 and B7alt
- Keep the part repetitive enough to practice against
- Avoid busy walking bass in the first version

## Drum direction

GarageBand drummer direction:

- Funk or roots kit
- Closed hi-hat sixteenths
- Kick locks with bass accents
- Snare on 2 and 4
- Ghost notes if available
- Fills only at section boundaries

## Guitar practice role

Practice:

- Wah on muted sixteenth-note strums
- Short chord stabs using three-note voicings
- Shell voicings on strings 4-3-2
- Rhythmic displacement: move the same stab by one sixteenth note
- Call-and-response between muted rhythm and sustained chord tones

## Constraints

- No lead playing for the first 10 minutes
- Keep wah motion tied to rhythm, not random sweeping
- Use partial chords only
- Record one pass and listen for pocket, muting, and pedal timing
- If the wah loses time, simplify the chord shape before changing the rhythm

## Variations

1. Make the B7alt bar more aggressive
2. Replace Dmaj9 with D6/9
3. Practice only offbeat stabs
4. Add a dotted-eighth delay hook after every 4-bar phrase
