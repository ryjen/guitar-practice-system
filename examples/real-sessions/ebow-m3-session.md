- Primary target: controlled activation, sustain, and string transitions
- Technique ID: ebow-articulation

- Target duration: about 30 minutes
- Stop condition: uncomfortable sustained level, clipping/runaway bloom, hand/shoulder fatigue, or loss of controlled activation after simplification

## Space constraint

Do not sustain continuously. Reserve at least one two-beat or longer silent/decay window in every four-bar phrase, and keep the upper register clear enough to hear attack and release behaviour.

## Prepare — about 5 minutes

- Verify battery and reliable operation.
- Find the activation hot spot on one string in normal mode.
- Check one soft attack and one deliberate release without delay/reverb.
- Hold one stable note for eight beats.
- Check peak level before trying harmonic mode.

Preparation success cue: activation is predictable enough to record a representative dry baseline.
