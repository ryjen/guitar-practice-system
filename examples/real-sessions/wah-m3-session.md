## Space constraint

Do not move the pedal continuously. Leave at least one complete phrase or two-bar window parked or bypassed between deliberate movement phrases.

## Prepare — about 5 minutes

- Recall heel, midpoint, and toe positions without looking.
- Match bypassed and engaged level.
- Play muted quarter notes with one repeatable small pedal movement.
- Check that toe position does not create an uncontrolled level spike.

Preparation success cue: one repeatable movement range is comfortable enough to compare dry and wah takes.
