# Jazz Noir E-Bow / Slide Background

## Intent

Create a slow jazz-coloured background for practicing E-Bow sustain, slide phrasing, and atmospheric lead lines.

## Core sound

Dark, spacious, late-night, minor-key. Jazz harmony, but played like ambient / post-punk texture rather than a standard.

## Settings

- Tempo: 72 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Key center: D minor
- Feel: slow brushed or side-stick groove
- Guitar role: E-Bow sustain, slide melody, sparse wah-filtered held notes
- Bass role: sparse root / fifth movement with chromatic approaches
- Drums: soft jazz kit, brush kit, or minimal side-stick pulse

## Chord loop

| Bar | Harmony | Function |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Dm9 | tonic colour |
| 2 | G13 | minor-to-dominant lift |
| 3 | Cmaj9 | soft release |
| 4 | A7alt | tension back to D minor |
| 5 | Dm9 | return |
| 6 | Bbmaj7#11 | cinematic colour |
| 7 | Em7b5 / A7alt | minor ii-V turnaround |
| 8 | Dm9 | resolution |

## Bass direction

- Keep the bass sparse and cinematic
- Use root / fifth movement as the default
- Add chromatic approaches into Dm9 and A7alt
- Avoid busy walking bass in the first version
- Leave enough low-end space for sustained guitar textures

## Drum direction

GarageBand drummer direction:

- Brush kit or soft jazz kit
- Low complexity
- Low-to-medium intensity
- Side-stick or brushed snare
- Ride pattern with space
- Small fills only every 8 bars

## Guitar practice role

Practice:

- E-Bow sustained chord tones
- Slide into 3rds, 7ths, and 9ths
- Slow bends into altered A7 tension
- Wah as a subtle vowel filter on held notes
- Sparse phrases that resolve clearly into Dm9

## Constraints

- No fast runs
- Mostly whole notes, half notes, and sparse eighth-note pickups
- Resolve altered tension intentionally
- Leave silence after phrases
- Record one pass and listen for pitch center, timing, and sustain control

## Variations

1. Replace A7alt with A7b9
2. Replace Bbmaj7#11 with Gm9
3. Add dotted-eighth delay to slide phrases
4. Practice only guide tones: 3rds and 7ths
