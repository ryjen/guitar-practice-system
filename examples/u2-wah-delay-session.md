# U2-Style Wah / Delay Session

## Intent

Practice rhythmic delay, upper-register hooks, and expressive wah accents without directly copying any song.

## Settings

- Tempo: 104 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Key: D major / B minor color
- Guitar role: chiming rhythmic texture
- Bass role: simple root motion
- Effects: dotted-eighth delay, light drive, optional wah accents

## Session

### 1. Warmup — delay grid

Play muted eighths on two strings while the delay supplies the bounce.

Constraint: keep the picking hand steady and let the delay fill space.

### 2. Main fragment

Use a two-string triad shape high on the neck. Play short syncopated hits instead of full strums.

Accent map:

```text
1   &   2   &   3   &   4   &
X       -   X       X   -
```

### 3. Wah variation

Use wah only on the second and fourth phrase endings.

Do not sweep constantly. Treat the pedal like punctuation.

### 4. Backing structure

| Section | Bars | Chords | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Intro | 4 | Dsus2 | delay hook only |
| Verse | 8 | D / A / Bm / G | bass roots, sparse guitar |
| Lift | 4 | G / A | increase delay feedback slightly |
| Chorus | 8 | D / G / Bm / A | open chord fragments |
