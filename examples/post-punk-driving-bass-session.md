# Post-Punk Driving Bass Session

## Intent

Build a guitar practice session around a driving bassline instead of guitar-first strumming.

## Settings

- Tempo: 142 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Key: E minor
- Bass role: constant eighth-note motor
- Drums: tight kick/snare, sparse cymbals
- Guitar role: clipped accents, short hooks, wah punctuation

## Bassline cell

```text
E E G E | D E B E
1 & 2 & | 3 & 4 &
```

## Guitar constraints

- no full open strumming
- use two-note shapes
- accent offbeats
- leave the bass in charge of movement

## Section map

| Section | Bars | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Intro | 4 | bass alone, drums enter bar 3 |
| A | 8 | clipped guitar accents |
| B | 8 | higher-register hook |
| Break | 4 | bass and toms only |
| A2 | 8 | return with wah accents |

## Practice pass

1. Play muted eighths with the bassline
2. Add offbeat two-note accents
3. Replace every second accent with a rest
4. Add wah punctuation only at phrase ends
5. Record one clean and one effected pass
