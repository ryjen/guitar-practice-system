# E-Bow Ambient Session

## Intent

Create a slow ambient rock texture using E-Bow as a drone, counterline, and transition device.

## Settings

- Tempo: 72 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Key: A minor
- Guitar role: sustain and atmosphere
- Bass role: sparse root / fifth movement
- Drums: minimal pulse or no drums at first

## Exercise 1 — drone bed

Hold A or E as a drone for 8 bars.

Constraints:

- no more than one pitch change every 2 bars
- slow volume movement
- listen for tuning and overtone stability

## Exercise 2 — counterline

Use only these notes:

```text
A C D E G
```

Build a sparse upper melody against an A minor drone.

## Exercise 3 — transition texture

Enter two bars before a section change, swell into the downbeat, then fade under the next section.

## Backing structure

| Section | Bars | Harmony | Texture |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Drone | 8 | Am | E-Bow root/fifth |
| Pulse | 8 | Am / G | bass enters |
| Lift | 8 | F / G / Am | counterline |
| Fade | 4 | Am | return to drone |

## What to generate next

Generate a bass-and-drums MIDI scaffold that leaves space for E-Bow sustain.
