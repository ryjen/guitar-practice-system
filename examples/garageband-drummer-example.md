# GarageBand Drummer Example

## Title

Post-punk bassline practice scaffold

## Global settings

- BPM: 142
- Time signature: 4/4
- Key / mode: E minor
- Drummer / kit: tight rock or indie kit
- Feel: straight eighths, controlled energy

## Section settings

| Section | Bars | Loudness | Complexity | Fills | Kit focus | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 4 | low | low | none | kick | bass enters first |
| A | 8 | mid | low-mid | low | kick/snare | clipped guitar accents |
| B | 8 | mid-high | mid | medium | snare/cymbal | higher hook enters |
| Break | 4 | mid | low | low | toms | strip cymbals back |
| A2 | 8 | high | mid | medium | full kit | wah accents at phrase ends |

## Kick / snare notes

- Kick should reinforce the bass root pulse
- Snare should stay direct and not too busy
- Avoid excessive ghost-note complexity in first version

## Guitar cue points

- Bar 5: first clipped accent
- Bar 13: higher hook
- Bar 25: wah response phrase

## Export notes

Create the drummer arrangement first, then record bass or MIDI bass. Guitar should enter after the groove feels stable.
