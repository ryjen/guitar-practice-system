# Modal Jazz Slide / Delay Background

## Intent

Create a spacious modal jazz background for slide guitar, delay hooks, and atmospheric melodic development.

## Core sound

Open, suspended, and slightly mysterious. More modal atmosphere than bebop. Works as a background for slide, E-Bow, and delay guitar.

## Settings

- Tempo: 84 BPM
- Meter: 6/8 or slow 12/8
- Key center: A Dorian
- Feel: floating pulse, light toms or brushed drums, no heavy backbeat
- Guitar role: slide melody, dotted-eighth delay motifs, E-Bow drone
- Bass role: pedal A with selective movement to D
- Drums: light percussion, brush kit, or tom-led pulse

## Bass direction

- Pedal A often
- Move to D only when the harmony needs lift
- Avoid walking bass
- Use long notes and occasional pickup figures
- Keep the bass hypnotic enough for slide phrasing practice

## Drum direction

GarageBand drummer direction:

- Light percussion, brush kit, or tom-forward kit
- 6/8 pulse
- Low intensity
- Toms and ride over snare
- Fills should feel like swells, not transitions

## Guitar practice role

Practice:

- Slide melodies targeting 9ths and 6ths
- Dorian phrases without blues-box autopilot
- Dotted-eighth delay motifs
- E-Bow drone on A or E against changing harmony
- Wah as slow filter movement on held notes

## Constraints

- No more than five notes per phrase
- Repeat motifs before varying them
- Use space after every phrase
- Build intensity by register and sustain, not speed
- Make the slide intonation audible by leaving the backing sparse

## Variations

1. Shift to A minor pentatonic for one pass, then restore Dorian colour
2. Add Cmaj7 to create a softer bridge
3. Make the D chord D7#11 for more tension
4. Practice only slide into chord tones
